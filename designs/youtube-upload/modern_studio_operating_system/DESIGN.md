## Brand & Style

This design system drives a modern AI social media workspace and Content OS. It combines the rigorous operational clarity of production tooling with the approachable, welcoming warmth of a creator-first companion. The interface evokes feelings of composure, organized velocity, deep focus, and creative agency—reminiscent of an upgraded, friendlier YouTube Studio built for multi-platform orchestration.

The design movement is **Modern Functional SaaS with Soft Editorial Accents**. It relies on crisp containment lines, deep midnight navigation anchors, airy canvas backdrops, and intentional splashes of energetic color reserved for actionable states, workflow velocity, and publication telemetry.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate hierarchy between platform chrome, content canvas, and generative intelligence layers:

- **Primary (`#4F46E5`)**: Primary interactive triggers, active timeline nodes, selected tabs, and AI prompt triggers. Interactive hover reaches `#4338CA`; subtle tints utilize `#EEF2FF` for soft fills, badge backgrounds, and selected table rows.
- **Secondary / Success (`#10B981`)**: Scheduled statuses, performance gains, published milestones, and system health.
- **Tertiary / Warning (`#F59E0B`)**: Content alerts, quota thresholds, processing notices, and draft revisions pending review.
- **Error / Danger (`#BA1A1A`)**: Critical failures and destructive prompts, paired with `#FFDAD6` for alert fills.
- **Platform Accent (`#FF0000`)**: Restricted exclusively to official YouTube channel metrics, sync connectors, and platform-specific badges.
- **Canvas Base (`#EEF2F7`)**: A cool, low-strain backdrop that makes cards pop cleanly without glaring contrast.
- **Sidebar & Chrome Canvas (`#0B1220`)**: Dense deep navy anchoring the global command rail and structural workspaces.
- **Surfaces (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure white containers bounded by crisp `#E2E8F0` structural perimeter lines.
- **Text Palette**:
  - Headings & High Emphasis: `#0B1C30`
  - Body & Medium Emphasis: `#464555`
  - Secondary & Muted Labels: `#777587`

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans governs the typography scale across all touchpoints, pairing modern geometric construction with organic humanist curves.

Numerical metrics, channel subscribers, and engagement rates rely on `title-md` and `headline-md` with tabular figures enabled (`tnum`) to eliminate layout jitter during live updates. Display scales adopt tight negative tracking to maintain commanding authority in dashboard overviews, while body and label styles prioritize open aperture and generous line heights to preserve scanning legibility inside dense content schedules and script editors.

## Layout & Spacing

The platform is structured around a multi-tier master-detail fluid grid architecture:
- **Navigation Dock / Left Rail**: Fixed width (260px expanded, 72px iconified) pinned directly against the viewport with `#0B1220` background.
- **Main Canvas**: A 12-column responsive fluid grid spanning inside the remaining viewport, flanked by `margin` padding.
- **Responsive Adaptations**:
  - **Desktop (>= 1200px)**: 12 columns, 24px gutters, 32px canvas margins. Multi-pane side-by-side editing (e.g., AI prompt inspector alongside video schedule preview).
  - **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8 columns, 16px gutters, 24px margins. Sidebar collapses to an icon bar; inspector overlays transform into off-canvas sliding drawers.
  - **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, 16px gutters, 16px margins. Bottom persistent navigation replaces the sidebar; multi-column content reflows into single stacked vertical streams.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy employs a calibrated blend of soft structural borders and layered atmospheric ambient shadows to avoid muddy contrasts over the `#EEF2F7` base:

- **Surface Level 0 (Base Canvas)**: `#EEF2F7`, zero elevation, completely non-interactive background.
- **Surface Level 1 (Default Containers & Cards)**: `#FFFFFF` fill, 1px perimeter border of `#E2E8F0`, and an ambient diffused shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(11, 28, 48, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(11, 28, 48, 0.03)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Interactive Trays)**: Subtle lift; border changes to `#CBD5E1` with ambient shadow: `0 8px 20px -4px rgba(11, 28, 48, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(11, 28, 48, 0.04)`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Flyouts, Popovers & Menus)**: `#FFFFFF` fill, border `#E2E8F0`, deep diffused float: `0 16px 32px -6px rgba(11, 28, 48, 0.12), 0 6px 12px -4px rgba(11, 28, 48, 0.06)`.
- **Surface Level 4 (Modals & Command Palettes)**: Centered structural sheet accompanied by backdrop blur `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)` with tint `rgba(11, 18, 32, 0.48)`.

## Shapes

The geometric vernacular balances operational structure with a modern, friendly touch:
- **Cards and Major Panels**: Fixed at `16px` radius (`rounded-lg`), establishing clear surface islands against the soft canvas.
- **Form Controls & Interactive Buttons**: Systematically unified at `12px` radius for smooth ergonomic targets.
- **Badges, Tags, and Avatars**: Fully rounded pills (`9999px`) to create clear geometric contrast against rectangular content cards.
- **Icon Enclosures**: `8px` or `10px` soft inner radii.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Indigo (`#4F46E5`), text `#FFFFFF`, height 44px (default) or 52px (featured hero actions), border radius 12px, horizontal padding 20px (24px for 52px). Hover state transitions to `#4338CA`. Active state scales subtly down (`scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary**: Surface `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, text `#0B1C30`, height matching primary. On hover, background shifts to `#F8F9FF` and border shifts to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Ghost / Tertiary**: Transparent fill, text `#464555`, 12px radius, hover fill `#EEF2FF` with text `#4F46E5`.

### Input Fields & Textareas
- **Container**: 44px height for single-line inputs (auto height for textareas), 12px border radius, background `#F8F9FF`, 1px border `#E2E8F0`.
- **States**:
  - Focus: Background switches to `#FFFFFF`, border changes to `#4F46E5`, 3px outer halo `rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.14)`.
  - Error: Border shifts to `#BA1A1A`, 3px halo `rgba(186, 26, 26, 0.12)`.
- **Meta Indicators**: Textareas and prompt inputs include real-time character/token counters pinned cleanly at bottom-right in `label-sm` color `#777587`.
- **Icon Integration**: Outline-style prefix/suffix icons rendered in 18px size, neutral `#777587`.

### Cards & Surfaces
- Base background `#FFFFFF`, 16px corner radius, 1px continuous border in `#E2E8F0`.
- Card headers feature clear separation through a 16px internal padding baseline, housing title (`title-md`), contextual badges, and action menus.

### Chips & Status Badges
- 28px height, full pill shape (`9999px`), 10px horizontal padding.
- Neutral variant: Background `#F1F5F9`, text `#464555`.
- Success / Published: Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#065F46`, 1px border `#A7F3D0`.
- AI Generated / Processing: Background `#EEF2FF`, text `#4F46E5`, 1px border `#C7D2FE`.
- Error: Background `#FFDAD6`, text `#BA1A1A`.

### Lists & Table Rows
- Enclosed within Level 1 white cards. Row heights are generous (56px-64px), divided by 1px horizontal borders in `#F1F5F9`.
- Row hover triggers smooth `#F8F9FF` background transitions. Drag handles, status chips, and quick action buttons reveal on row hover.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- 20px dimension with 6px border radius (checkbox) or circular (radio), border 1.5px solid `#CBD5E1`, background `#FFFFFF`.
- Checked state: Solid `#4F46E5` fill with crisp white check/bullet graphic and indigo micro-glow.

### Iconography
- Clean geometric outline icons (Lucide/Material Symbols style), 1.75px stroke width, square proportions (16px, 20px, 24px) centered inside interaction targets.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Content Pipeline Nodes**: Visual cards tracking multi-stage post lifecycle (Drafting -> Scripting -> Rendering -> Scheduled) with connected indigo indicator threads.
- **AI Assist Prompt Bar**: Docked command-input surface styled in `#FFFFFF` with glowing 1px border gradient using primary indigo tints, featuring a dedicated instant action trigger.
- **Channel Stat Pill**: Dedicated metric capsule highlighting connected platforms (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, X) with authentic branding icons and high-contrast performance numbers.
